- **UPIC:** Aleksi Eeben / CNCD, border-color raster graphics and UPIC 1.3 ([original UPIC release](https://csdb.dk/release/?id=263889)). The player's current renderer adapts the GPLv3 [UPIC library](https://github.com/xahmol/ultimate-uci-oscar64/blob/main/include/ultimate_upic_lib.c) maintained by **Xander Mol / xahmol**, which publishes Aleksi's source with his permission. Earlier versions drew on [Mandelbrot UPIC](https://github.com/xahmol/mandelbrot-upic).

Rigg for color matching, with [implementation guidance](https://hajim.rochester.edu/ece/sites/gsharma/ciede2000/) by G. Sharma, W. Wu and E. N. Dalal.

SID audio uses Sebastian Jastrzebski's [reSID-rs](https://github.com/binaryfields/resid-rs), GPL-3.0-or-later, with translations of Dag Lem's VICE reSID, GPL-2.0-or-later. Vendor licenses and provenance are retained alongside those sources.

The [original sources, notices and WGSL exporter](site/crt/source.tar.gz), [GPL text](site/crt/COPYING-GPL-2.0.txt), and [pinned revision and hashes](site/crt/UPSTREAM.json) accompany the browser conversions. [Rebuild instructions](site/crt/UPSTREAM.md) describe the sampling adaptation.
