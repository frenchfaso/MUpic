MUpic

(Motion UPIC)

16 colors is all you need!

Up and Down select a video. Return plays it. Space pauses or resumes. Escape returns to the list.

MUpic is a video format for the C64 Ultimate — up to 360×200, 16 colors at 16.7 fps (PAL).

Made possible thanks to the invaluable work of Aleksi Eeben (UPIC) and Joel Yliluoma / Bisqwit (color selection and dithering).
